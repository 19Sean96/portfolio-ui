## Decisions

### 1. Line endings

- **What:** added `.gitattributes` with `* text=auto eol=lf`.
- **Why:** with `core.autocrlf=true` (Sean's global git setting) every file checked out CRLF, and Prettier's `endOfLine: lf` failed all 62. The files in git were already LF; only the checkout was wrong.
- **Rejected:** turning off `endOfLine` in Prettier, or running `prettier --write` (that would only fix this checkout, and loosens a check).
- **Undo:** revert the commit.

### 2. No author notes on public pages; drafts stay on the dev server

- **What:** removed the visible "Placeholder: …" paragraphs from AV and Berine, and the first sentence of the Berine diagram caption ("Placeholder diagram from the ops desk docs."). Each note now sits as a code comment beside the copy it was about, so nothing Sean wrote is lost. The `/work` lede lost "Entries marked draft are placeholders." `validateProject` now fails a published entry whose copy says "placeholder" or "tbd", and a test covers it.
- **What:** entries with `placeholder: true` (Integralife, Next experiment) show on `pnpm dev` with their draft mark, and a production build leaves them out of every list and their pages 404. A test stubs `DEV` off to check this. In a build, home shows two featured projects, Berine and AV.
- **What:** GIF Search's GitHub Pages site answers 404, so its link is marked `dead` ("may be offline").
- **Why:** AGENTS.md says to show only built work, and these notes read as unfinished to a visitor. Nothing was reworded: copy was only removed.
- **Rejected:** deleting `next-experiment.ts` and the Integralife entry (Sean's slots; flip `placeholder` off when the copy is real). Rewriting Integralife's body (that would be new claims).
- **Kept:** Campaign Builder's "may be offline" mark. Its page shell answers 200, but whether the campaign still loads its data was not checked.
- **Undo:** revert the commit.

### 3. AV source links hidden while the repo is private

- **What:** `github.com/19Sean96/av-series` is private (`gh repo view` on 2026-11-18), so the five "Source" links and "Source on GitHub" on `/work/av-series` answer 404 to a visitor. They now hang off one flag, `repoIsPublic = false`, at the top of `src/content/projects/av-series.ts`.
- **Why:** a portfolio link that 404s reads as broken. Sean added these links on purpose in #2, so this is one isolated commit and one flag.
- **Rejected:** deleting the links (he may make the repo public); leaving them (they fail for everyone but him).
- **Undo:** set `repoIsPublic = true` once the repo is public, or revert this commit.

### 4. HTML in canvas draws again, and falls back when it cannot

- **What:** `/lab/html-in-canvas` showed an empty bordered box in Chrome 157. The browser has `drawElementImage`, so the capability check passed, but the proposal changed shape: the canvas now needs `content="drawable"` and each child `drawable` (the old `layoutsubtree` alone throws `InvalidStateError`). The piece sets both shapes and draws again.
- **What:** a lab piece can now call `ctx.fallback(reason)`. The page drops the piece and shows its still with that sentence, the same way it does for a missing capability. HTML in canvas calls it after about a second of frames that would not draw, instead of swallowing the error forever. Checked by making `drawElementImage` throw in the page: the still and reason appeared, the canvas went away.
- **Rejected:** removing the piece, or only fixing the attribute (the next change to the proposal would blank it again).
- **Undo:** revert the commit.

### 5. Reading measure, and a diagram you can read on a phone

- **What:** on `/work/berine-metabolic` the flow diagram's width stretched the `.prose` grid track, so every paragraph ran about 1,070px (142 characters a line). `.prose` now uses `minmax(0, 1fr)`, so the diagram overflows wide to the right and the text keeps its measure.
- **What:** `--reading-width` went from 42rem to 36rem. At 42rem body copy ran 84 to 90 characters a line on About and AV; 36rem gives about 70 to 75.
- **What:** under 52rem the diagram reflows to one column in edge order (a second SVG; CSS shows one, and the hidden one never plays). Before, phones scaled the wide diagram to about 0.75× and the detail text rendered near 8px in a box that scrolled sideways. Nodes are a little wider (190 units, was 175) and detail text a step up (0.75rem, was 0.6875rem), because detail lines touched or crossed their box edge. A label on a vertical edge sits beside the line.
- **Rejected:** keeping the sideways scroll with bigger text (still a scroll inside a scroll on phones).
- **Captures:** `before/work_berine-metabolic.*`, `after/work_berine-metabolic.*`.
- **Undo:** revert the commit.

### 6. Keyboard, landmarks and target sizes

- **What:** the card focus ring was clipped. `.card` hides overflow for its media, and the ring is an outer box-shadow on the link that fills it (`before/state-focus-card.desktop.jpg` shows no ring). The card now wears the ring when its link has `:focus-visible` (`after/state-focus-card.desktop.jpg`).
- **What:** the 404 page's `<main>` had no `id="main"`, so the skip link went nowhere there. It has one now.
- **What:** `/work` and `/lab` went from h1 straight to h3. Cards take a `level` prop and render h2 under a page h1; a `card-title` class keeps them looking the same.
- **What:** the AV "in progress" mark stretched across the whole row. It now sizes to its text. Card text starts at the top, so the lab cards' meta rows line up. Lab sliders use the accent colour, not the browser's blue.
- **What:** standalone links (wordmark, nav, crumbs, "All work", project links, AV piece links) are at least 44px tall on every pointer, not only coarse ones. Sean asked for a 44px safe zone (2026-07-15). The header lost 16px of padding to keep its height.
- **Undo:** revert the commit.

### 7. Reduced motion reaches the clips and the grain

- **What:** project clips played on load, all five on `/work` at once, with reduced motion on too, and each downloaded in full (2.4MB) because autoplay overrides `preload`. A clip now plays only while a quarter of it is on screen, and never in calm mode, where it holds its first frame. Checked: at 1440×900 the two on-screen clips play and the three below the fold stay paused; with reduced motion all five stay paused.
- **What:** the grain pass re-seeded every frame even in calm mode, because any change in time re-rolls the hash. Calm mode now holds the grain still.
- **Kept, a judgment call:** the stage itself still drifts in calm mode at 0.15× speed. `clock.ts` documents that as the design ("scales time down instead of stopping") and the tests cover it, and Sean's 2026-10-01 ruling for hero motion was to obey reduced motion and settle, not to freeze. If he wants calm mode to stop the stage outright, it is one line in `createClock` (`CALM_SCALE = 0`) plus its test.
- **Not done:** clips have no pause control. They are muted, sit inside a card link, and stop off screen and under reduced motion; a visible pause button inside the link would be a second target in one card.
- **Undo:** revert the commit.

### 8. Lit depth instead of outlines, inside the existing look

This is a refinement, not a redesign: same dark stage, same Inter type, same layout bones. It applies Sean's standing calls: lit depth over flat fills, space instead of dividers, no AI-default marks, physical motion.

- **Depth:** the 11 hairline borders on cards, AV pieces, the diagram frame, lab controls, project media and lab stills are gone. Raised surfaces get a top light wash, a bright 1px inset top edge and a two-step shadow (`--surface-light`, `--edge-light`, `--shadow-1`, `--shadow-2`, `--raised`, `--raised-high` in `tokens.css`). An unbuilt AV piece sits flat and unlit instead of dashed.
- **Space for dividers:** About's roles were split by hairlines; they now stand apart by 2rem of space, and role headings step down to a new `--text-subheading` (1.125rem) so "Roles" outranks them.
- **Status marks, not pills:** status, draft, sketch, in progress and "may be offline" were outlined capsules. They are now a small lit dot in the status colour plus the word, in the spirit of the pseudo-LED indicators Sean likes. Lab buttons were pill-shaped; they are raised keys now, lit on top, that sink when pressed.
- **Motion:** card hover lifts 4px on a spring curve (`--ease-spring`, a CSS `linear()` curve that overshoots 4% and settles) and the media inside eases up 3%; a press settles it back. Both drop to 0ms under reduced motion through `--duration-spring`.
- **Header:** the wordmark sat at the 40px gutter while the content column started at 184px on desktop. The header's edges now follow the page column.
- **Footer:** every page ended in a dead end. A footer now says what the found count means (the old `title` text, which touch screens never showed) and links Sean's GitHub (`github.com/19Sean96`, the account every project repo already links). On phones the header drops its count to stay on one row; the footer carries it.
- **Type:** card lines, piece lines, captions and the footer use a new `--text-small` (0.9375rem, was the 0.8125rem label size), since Sean wants generous text.
- **No stand-in hero:** a project with no capture no longer leads its page with a large gradient blur (Berine). It leads with its words and its diagram. On cards, the gradient face now carries the project's name set large and lit in its own two hues, instead of two blurred blobs.
- **Home:** the featured work grid fills its row (`auto-fit`), so the two featured projects in a build (Berine, AV) share the width instead of leaving a third slot empty.
- **Rejected, for Sean:** the design review proposed a bigger move (see "Ideas for Sean" at the end): `/work` as an asymmetric "now" block plus a compact archive list, or the AV pieces playing live behind one large title per project, like a set list. That is a redesign of the page layer, so it is written up here and not built.
- **Undo:** revert the commit.

### 9. Footer at the bottom, a header that frosts, words that hold over a live scene

- **What:** `body` is a flex column and `.page` grows, so the footer rests at the bottom of a short page (404, lab pieces). `.page` keeps its rows at the top.
- **What:** the header is open at the top of the page and frosts (72% ground, 14px blur) over the first 6rem of scroll, through a CSS scroll timeline. No script. Browsers without scroll timelines keep the old fade.
- **What:** on lab pages, the crumb, title, lede, header and footer carry a soft dark halo, because a piece can paint the stage bright (gradient field's salmon, audio field's ring).
- **What:** a page's crumb, title and lede sit 1rem closer together, so they read as one group.
- **Undo:** revert the commit.

### 10. Titles first on cards, and grids without orphans

- **What:** the status, years or tech list sat above each card title, reading as an eyebrow and knocking titles out of line when one card had two meta lines. Meta now follows the title and line.
- **What:** `/work` follows its own lede: a **Current** grid of large cards, then an **Archive** grid of smaller ones (one row of five at 1440). In the build the old single grid left one card alone on a third row. The two short h2s reuse the lede's words.
- **What:** lab grids fit three across from tablet width up, so the third piece no longer sits alone at 820.
- **Undo:** revert the commit.
